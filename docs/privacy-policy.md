# Privacy Policy for Memory Game

*Last updated: 6 September 2026*

Memory Game (package `com.snatik.matches`, published on Google Play by Roman Kushnarenko) is a
simple memory card game for children and their families.

## What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting
SDKs, and no third-party services. It does not ask for any permissions and never accesses the
internet on its own.

## What the app stores

The only data the app keeps is your game progress: the best star rating and the best time for each
theme and difficulty, and whether sound is on or off. This is stored on your device only. It may be
included in your device's standard Android backup (Google Drive backup or device-to-device
transfer), which is controlled by your device settings, not by the app.

Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

## Links to other services

The settings screen has a "Rate this" button that opens the app's page in the Google Play Store app
or in your browser. Google's own privacy policy applies there.

## Children

The app is designed to be safe for children. It collects no personal information from anyone,
including children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at the same address and the date above is
updated.
