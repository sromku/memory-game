# 1.4.0 (versionCode 1012)

The first upload since 2019 (1.3.0, versionCode 1011, was built but never uploaded), so the Play
text describes the whole comeback.

Play Console "What's new" (under 500 characters):

```
The game is back, bigger! Four worlds: Animals, Monsters, Emojis and the new Ocean, each with six roads of 100 rounds and stars to collect. Every fifth round is a surprise mini-game, seven in all. A friends album: every ten rounds a new friend joins, tap one to meet it by name. A parents' corner behind a hold-to-open gate. Speaks 21 languages, switchable in the settings. Works on phones and tablets. No ads, no internet, nothing collected.
```

Included since 1.3.0: twenty translations with a language picker and localised title art, the map
as the hub with theme and difficulty pickers, progress per theme, roads of 100 rounds, five more
mini-games (What changed?, Shadow match, Odd one out, Shopping list, Peek and find), the friends
album with names and the rounds-to-meet popup, the parents' corner with privacy policy and reset,
the Ocean world, right-to-left layouts, clean ribbon art, a theme picker that shows it scrolls.
Data safety and privacy declarations are unchanged: progress stays on the device, no permissions.
