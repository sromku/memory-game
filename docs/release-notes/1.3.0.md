# 1.3.0 (versionCode 1011)

Play Console "What's new" (under 500 characters):

```
A whole road to travel! Every difficulty is now a road of 40 rounds with stars to collect, and the next road opens as you play. Tap Play to jump straight into your next round. Every fifth round is a surprise: "Who was here?" (spot who left the party) and "Follow the song" (repeat the animals' tune). Confetti and hopping friends celebrate every finished round, and a friend greets you on the menu each day. No ads, no internet, nothing collected.
```

Included since 1.2.0: progress model and store with migration of earlier stars, level map, quick play, celebration, two mini-games with synthesised notes, friend of the day, round label, screen-reader access to the map and mini-games, pre-rendered UI art. Data safety and privacy declarations are unchanged: progress stays on the device.
