# Parents' corner

The settings popup's fourth row is "For parents". A tap only shows "Hold to open"; holding the row
for a second and a half opens the corner, a gate a small child does not pass by accident and that
needs no reading. Inside: the privacy policy link, and "Start over", which erases every road,
star and friend on the device, and itself must be held for two seconds while a bar fills. A note
says what it does. Sound and language settings are untouched by a reset.

`HoldToOpen` is the small helper that turns a view into a hold-to-act control; screen readers
reach the same actions through a long click.
