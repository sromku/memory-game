Privacy Policy

Memory Game for Android (package com.snatik.matches), published by Roman Kushnarenko. Last updated 6 September 2026.

Memory Game is a simple memory card game for children and their families.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party services. It does not request any permissions and never connects to the internet on its own.

What the app stores

The only data the app keeps is your game progress: the best star rating and the best time for each theme and difficulty, and whether sound is on or off. This is stored on your device only. It may be included in your device's standard Android backup (Google Drive backup or device-to-device transfer), which is controlled by your device settings, not by the app. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Links to other services

The settings screen has a "Rate this" button that opens the app's page in the Google Play Store, and a link to this page. Google's own privacy policy applies on Google Play.

Children

The app is designed to be safe for children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address and the date above is updated.
